# SocioCyberneering Inc.

![A city and a network](img/sce-city.svg)

**SocioCyberneering Inc. Communication Cooperations** is a project at the meeting point of sociocybernetics, general semantics and the ideas of The Venus Project as Jacque Fresco developed them until 2017.

## Sociocybernetics

Cybernetics studies how systems steer themselves through feedback. Sociocybernetics applies the same view to society: people, communities and cities are systems that can either swing out of control or find balance. Good connections between people are an engineering task, just like a good circuit.

## Ideas of The Venus Project

- the scientific method instead of opinions for social problems;
- a resource-based economy: start from what the planet actually has and what people need;
- automation that frees people from routine labour;
- cities designed as a whole, such as Fresco's circular cities;
- precise language as the basis of understanding (here Fresco is close to Korzybski).

## Jacque Fresco (1916–2017)

American industrial designer and futurist. Together with Roxanne Meadows he created The Venus Project in Florida: a research centre, city models and lectures on how technology and science can work for all people.

## What this has to do with CyberCord

- **No central servers**: no owner, no ads, no single point of failure. The network is its participants.
- **Background mode**: everyone who stays online helps the network work. Cooperation, not competition.
- **Topic servers**: SCE, Atom-Generator, Automation, AI Design, ART, Creativity, General-Semantics, Archives, Help.
- **"Words matter"**: no censorship and no bans, only an honest hint to yourself.
